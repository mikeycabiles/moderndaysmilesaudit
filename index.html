01 — Executive Summary

Executive Summary

Three funnel variants reviewed — the Control, the Original, and the Newest — each evaluated across landing page structure, conversion architecture, creative strategy, and ad execution. All findings are based on initial review and would be validated through structured A/B testing before any permanent changes are implemented.

The Control is the strongest performer by structure. The Original and Newest funnels carry more visual sophistication but introduce friction points that, in a paid social environment, are likely suppressing conversion. The most consistent opportunity across all three is the shift to a mobile-first, single-page funnel strategy with a stronger lead capture architecture.

02 — Funnel Analysis

Control Funnel

The most conversion-ready of the three funnels. Its structure aligns well with paid traffic best practices — lead form above the fold, a credibility-first layout, and simplified content hierarchy. The task here is refinement, not rebuilding.

Landing Page — What Is Working

Strongest Structure
"Don't Overpay" callout — Effective credibility pre-frame before pricing is introduced. Builds viewer confidence early in the scroll journey.
Dr. Vivek named prominently at the top — Practitioner authority at the most-viewed section of the page. Correct placement for high-ticket healthcare.
Lead form above the fold — Forms above the fold capture up to 3× more leads than scroll-dependent CTAs, particularly for cold traffic.
Sub-5-minute educational video — Deep enough to build trust, short enough to hold attention before the CTA. Optimal warm-up length for a landing page.
Warranty, tax deductible & water dental implants callouts — Effective re-engagement anchors for viewers who hesitate after encountering pricing. Well-placed objection handlers throughout the scroll.

Landing Page — Priority Improvements

Priority FixesTest
Domain and URL structure must be updated. The current domain significantly undermines brand credibility. Recommended migration to a branded subdomain such as dentalimplants.icotdentalcare.com, newpatient.icotdentalcare.com, or special.icotdentalcare.com. Domain trust directly impacts click-through and form submission rates.
Test a two-stage form. Stage 1 collects contact information only (name, phone, email). Stage 2 presents qualifying questions. If a visitor drops off at stage 2, their contact is already captured for follow-up. This structure allows for more qualifying questions — including FICO if legally required — without sacrificing top-of-funnel lead volume. Also test a pricing-intent radio question in stage 2: "How much is a great smile worth to you?" with ranges from $0 to $40k+ per set, giving the sales team an intent signal before outreach without introducing price resistance at the top of the funnel.
Before/after transformation gallery needs borders. No clear visual separation between cases — some groupings appear to be the same patient. Minor fix with a meaningful impact on perceived credibility.
Jake Ryan's review is four scrolls long. Truncate with a "Read more" toggle, or give it a full-width container. Breaks visual rhythm in the social proof section as-is.
No secondary CTA near Dr. Vivek's about section. Viewers who scroll deep without converting need a frictionless path back to the form. Add a CTA button or embedded mini-form in this section.
Test a hard link vs. soft link in Meta Ads. A soft link keeps users in the in-app browser where one tap exits. A hard link routes to the native browser, removing the easy escape hatch. Given the page's length, this could meaningfully improve scroll depth and form completions.
Pricing display should be market-tested. High-ticket pricing without adequate warm-up may increase abandonment. This is a market I have less direct experience with — whether upfront pricing helps or hurts conversion here is something I'd need to test rather than assume.

Ad Creative

Brand CohesiveFormat Gaps
Consistent visual identity across ad variations — Cohesion builds recognition across the feed, especially important for high-ticket services where repeated exposure drives the decision process.
Correctly targeted toward Facebook — Dental implant ICP (45+, homeowners, higher household income) indexes strongly on Facebook over Instagram or TikTok.
A 22-minute video ad is unlikely to be completing. Paid video watch time drops sharply past 2 minutes. Avg. paid view duration: <1 min. Front-load the value proposition or segment into a series. Add mid-roll CTAs every 60–90 seconds for content that intentionally runs long.
No testimonial in rotation. Patient testimonials are among the highest-converting formats in healthcare — they reduce perceived risk and provide social proof in a format that feels native to the feed.
Short-form content is underrepresented. Build a 30–60 second creative tier for awareness and retargeting. Lower CPMs, useful for warming audiences before longer educational content.
03 — Funnel Analysis

Original Funnel

The strongest overall visual foundation of the three. Clear understanding of brand presentation. However, its multi-page website structure is fundamentally mismatched for cold paid traffic. The aesthetic is right; the architecture is not.

Landing Page

Premium AestheticHigh Friction
Core Structural Issue
Do not direct paid ad traffic to this website. Multi-page sites create multiple exit points and allow the viewer to self-navigate away from the conversion goal before submitting their information. On Facebook and Instagram, users are there to be entertained — not to purchase. Every click away from the lead form is a potential lost lead. The primary goal of any paid traffic landing page is to capture contact information. A lead in the CRM who says "no" can be reactivated; a visitor who never fills out the form cannot be followed up with at all.
What to Preserve
Visual design language and premium presentation — Significantly more polished than the Control. These design elements should be extracted and applied to a single-page funnel architecture.
Forms present across multiple pages — The intent to capture is already built in. Consolidate into one optimized flow.
Recommended Action
Build a dedicated single-page landing page using the visual design of this site, structured like the Control's conversion architecture. Isolates the user in a frictionless environment and removes the ability to self-navigate away from the conversion goal.

Ad Creative

Large LibraryExecution Gaps
Large library provides more testing surface area — More creative variations allow for faster A/B iteration and give the algorithm more to optimize against.
Jocelyn's 30-second testimonial is the right format — Correct length and style. This is the direction the entire library should move toward.
Captions missing on multiple ads. Roughly ~85% of Facebook videos are watched on mute. Uncaptioned video loses the majority of viewers before a single message lands. Apply retroactively to all existing creative and make it a production standard going forward.
No CTA at the end of most or all testimonials. Every video ad must close with a directed next step. Without a CTA, the viewer has consumed the content with nowhere to go — the lead is lost at the finish line.
Caption styling currently draws attention to the fact that it looks like an ad. Consistent brand colors, voice, and tone create subconscious recognition — every time someone sees the palette, it reinforces the brand connection and keeps the practice top of mind. Caption design that clashes with the brand identity breaks that association and signals to the viewer that this is a paid placement, which increases resistance. Captions should feel native to the creative and consistent with the brand system.
Top-of-funnel ads must look like organic content. With 82% of users on mobile, Facebook and Instagram users are there for entertainment — they are a cold audience, not a warm one. The goal is to earn their attention first, convert that attention into a view, and then convert that view into a form fill. Landscape video immediately reads "advertisement" in a mobile feed, causing reflexive scrolling before the content can land. Vertical (9:16) or square (1:1) formats blend naturally into the Reels environment and give the creative a chance to stop the scroll.
Purple/green coloring and emoji-heavy creative erodes credibility at this price point. Recommend testing two versions of the same testimonial — clean vs. current styling — at ~$20/day per ad set before removing it entirely.
Ad copy is category-generic. Same anecdotal pain-point framing used by every competitor in this space. Lead with a specific insight or data point instead. The creative that stops the scroll doesn't look like every other healthcare ad.
More doctor-to-camera content is needed. In high-ticket healthcare, the connection to the actual provider is a primary conversion driver. A face reduces perceived risk in a way a brand logo cannot. Consistently validated across healthcare verticals and adjacent industries such as fitness.

05 — Infrastructure

Technical & CRM Recommendations

The current funnel infrastructure has clear limitations around domain credibility, page builder flexibility, and lead management. The following addresses the underlying technical stack needed to support the conversion improvements outlined above.

GoHighLevel CRM
Full-featured CRM and landing page platform with direct hands-on experience. Unlimited subaccounts under one plan — one per dental location — covering all six practices plus the surgical office in a unified system with shared automations and lead flows.
Branded Subdomain Migration
Replace the current landing page domain with a subdomain under the primary ICOT website. Recommended: dentalimplants.icotdentalcare.com or newpatient.icotdentalcare.com. Domain trust directly impacts form submission rates.
HIPAA Compliance Layer
GoHighLevel offers a HIPAA compliance add-on. Given the personal health intent data collected through dental landing pages, HIPAA-compliant data handling is a risk management priority and may be legally required depending on how patient data is stored and transmitted.
Dental-Specific CRM Evaluation
Depending on existing practice management software (Dentrix, Eaglesoft, etc.), a purpose-built dental CRM may integrate more natively with clinical workflows. Recommend scheduling discovery calls with 1–2 dental-specific vendors to compare capabilities before committing long-term.

Note on Website Hosting: GoHighLevel's website builder runs on a WordPress backend, which carries well-documented security vulnerabilities that are difficult to maintain without dedicated cybersecurity oversight. Recommendation is to use GoHighLevel exclusively for landing pages and CRM functions — not as the primary practice website host. Especially important given HIPAA compliance obligations.

06 — Action Plan

Priority Action Items

The following items represent the recommended sequence of changes across all three funnels and the underlying infrastructure. Each should be validated through structured testing before being treated as a permanent change.

1
Migrate landing page to a branded subdomain
Replace the current domain with a subdomain under icotdentalcare.com. A credibility fix that costs nothing and directly impacts trust before a visitor reads a word of copy.
2
Add captions to all existing video ads
Roughly 85% of Facebook videos are watched on mute. Uncaptioned video is the single most consistent and easily fixable performance gap across all three ad libraries.
3
Add a CTA to the end of every video ad
No video should end without a directed next step. Applies to all existing testimonials, educational content, and any doctor-facing creative currently in rotation.
4
Build a single-page funnel using the Newest funnel's visual design
Combine the Control's conversion architecture with the Newest funnel's visual design language. Target state for all paid social traffic. Build on GoHighLevel for maximum flexibility and tracking control.
5
Produce vertical-format video creative for all key ad types
Shoot or reformat testimonials, doctor-facing content, and educational videos in 9:16 for Reels placement. Reduces CPMs and increases native-feel engagement on mobile.
6
Produce doctor-to-camera video content
At least 2–3 pieces of direct-to-camera content featuring the treating physician. Personal connection to the provider is a documented conversion driver in high-ticket healthcare. Prioritize authenticity over production polish.
7
Implement and test the two-stage lead form
Stage 1: contact info only. Stage 2: qualifying questions including FICO (if legally required) and the pricing-intent radio question. Run against the current single-stage form to measure impact on lead volume and quality.
8
Evaluate and select CRM platform
Schedule discovery calls with GoHighLevel and at least one dental-specific CRM vendor. Confirm HIPAA compliance requirements, subaccount structure, and integration with existing practice management software before committing.
07 — Closing

Closing Notes

Everything outlined in this audit is a starting point — a set of informed hypotheses built from direct observation of the current funnel and ad ecosystem. None of these recommendations should be treated as permanent directives. Every significant change should be A/B tested with appropriate budget controls before full rollout.

The foundation is already strong. The Control is performing for a reason. The goal of this process is to take what is working, remove the points of friction that are likely costing leads, and build toward a funnel system that can scale across all six practices with a consistent, credible, and conversion-optimized presence.

I am prepared to begin executing on these changes immediately and look forward to continuing the conversation around next steps, testing frameworks, and CRM selection.
